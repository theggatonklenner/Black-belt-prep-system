# Stage 9: Reports and exports

No database update is needed.

## Where to find it

- **More > Reports and exports**: every report, plus Export everything for admins.
- **Export** button on the **Students** screen: the student register.
- **Export** button on the **Attendance** screen: attendance by session for the cycle you're looking at.
- **Grading list** and **Certificate list** buttons on those screens (also listed under Reports).

## Reports

Each report has filters, ready-made versions, a column picker (tick to add, ↑ ↓ to reorder), a preview and saved templates.

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Student register | Student ID, names, belt, next belt, Dan number, active, date added, ID card status, cycles, notes |
| Attendance by session | Each session and the sign-off (P, A, E), attended, absent, excused, %, requirement met, paid sessions |
| Attendance history | Every student in every cycle: attended, sign-off, requirement met, readiness, grading result |
| Readiness | Status, reason, notes, next action, last reviewed; ready-made: not ready, needs more preparation, awaiting assessment |
| Payments (admins and payments role) | Fee, option, amount due, paid, balance, status, due date (grading fees only); ready-made: outstanding, overdue, partially paid, fully paid |
| Follow-up list | Everyone with a readiness, attendance or payment issue, with the issues listed (amounts only for admins and payments role) |
| ID cards | Card printed, in print queue, reprint needed, not printed, or no card linked |
| Grading list, Certificate list | As in stages 6 and 7 |

## Download formats

- **Excel** (.xlsx), with the report title and date at the top and sized columns.
- **CSV**, which opens in Excel or Google Sheets (File > Import in Sheets).
- **Print / PDF**: a clean printable page; choose "Save as PDF" in the print screen for a PDF.

## Export everything (admins)

One Excel workbook with a sheet for each table: Students, Cycles, Sessions, Attendance, Readiness, Grading, Certificates, and (for admins with payments access) Fees and Payment transactions. Useful as a backup or to share with the committee.

Money is only ever included for admins and the payments role.
